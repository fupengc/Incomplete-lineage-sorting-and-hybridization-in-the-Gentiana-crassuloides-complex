# Incomplete-lineage-sorting-and-hybridization-in-the-Gentiana-crassuloides-complex
Phylogenomic and morphological data of Gentiana crassuloide complex, including G. crassuloide, G. curviphylla, G. daofuensis, G. bryoides, and G. nyingchiensis, is included here. In detail, single-copy orthologous genes, complete chloroplast genome sequences, and morphological data from the complex are collected.

Data Details: Nuclear genes (941).zip: including the 941 single-copy orthologous genes from Gentiana crassuloides complex.

Plastome of G. crassuloides complex (with one IR).fas: including the chloroplast genomes (after removing one IR) from Gentiana crassuloides complex.

Morphological data of G. crassuloides complex.txt: containing the morphological data from Gentiana crassuloides complex measured at population level.
